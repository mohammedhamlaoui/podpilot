# PodPilot Design Context

## Brand

PodPilot

## Style Direction

PodPilot should feel like a premium modern SaaS product: clean, light, focused, simple, and professional. The interface should support repeated dashboard work without feeling heavy or decorative.

## Inspiration

Use the polish and restraint of Linear and Vercel as inspiration: crisp layout, strong spacing, clear hierarchy, quiet surfaces, and confident details.

## Color

- Main color: purple `#7C3AED`
- Background: soft white and light gray
- Cards: white surfaces with rounded corners, subtle borders, and soft shadows
- Text: dark navy or near-black with clear contrast

## Component Direction

- Sidebar: minimal icon sidebar with a purple active state
- Dashboard: compact, premium, and easy to scan
- Onboarding card: clear and useful, not oversized
- Stats cards: simple, readable, and aligned
- Platform overview: clear Redbubble and TeePublic sections
- Auth pages: centered, no scrolling, with the animated SVG logo preserved

## Avoid

- Childish gradients
- Too many colors
- Heavy shadows
- Messy spacing
- Decorative clutter
- Redesigning the whole app without being asked

## Design Rule

Do not redesign the whole app unless explicitly asked. Preserve the existing PodPilot style and improve only what needs polish, clarity, spacing, or consistency.
