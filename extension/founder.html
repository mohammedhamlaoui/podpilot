Founder intro

I'm Mohammed, an 18-year-old developer from Morocco. I built PodPilot to help print-on-demand creators save time when preparing listings, managing designs, and uploading to platforms like Redbubble and TeePublic.

Why I built PodPilot

I started working on PodPilot because repetitive upload tasks take too much time. My goal is to make the process faster, cleaner, and easier for creators, while keeping users in control of their content.

Trust and privacy note

PodPilot is built with privacy and security in mind. The tool is designed to help with workflow automation, not to take ownership of your work.
